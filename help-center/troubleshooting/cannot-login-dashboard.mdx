---
title: Cannot log in to dashboard
description: Resolve login issues in Clarity OPS.
---

## Symptoms

- Login fails with "Invalid credentials"
- Password reset email not received

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check credentials">
    Ensure your email and password are correct. Caps Lock may affect entry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset password">
    Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Check your spam folder for the reset link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Still having issues?">
    <Callout kind="info">
      Contact support at support@clarityops.com for help.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>
