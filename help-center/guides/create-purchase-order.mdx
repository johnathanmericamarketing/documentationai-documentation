## Create a Purchase Order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Purchase Orders">
    Go to the "Purchase Orders" tab in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new order">
    Click "New Purchase Order".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in details">
    Choose your supplier, add products, quantities, and set pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Confirm all order info is correct. Click "Submit Order".
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your new purchase order will appear in the Orders list.
