## Overview

Add your first product easily in Clarity OPS.

## Steps

1. Log in to your Clarity OPS dashboard
2. Go to the "Products" menu
3. Click "Add Product"
4. Fill in product name, SKU, and details
5. Set the initial price and inventory
6. Click "Save"

Your product is now listed and ready to manage!
